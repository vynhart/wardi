# Cart Page

The HTML draft for cart page is on cart-draft.html

# Purpose

When user click on "View Cart" button on the store page,
they will open this screen to review the list of products
they have added to cart and to proceed to the next step
to checkout.

In this screen, they can:
- Decrease / increase the qty of products they want to checkout
    - Product with 0 qty wouldn't be included in checkout.
- Click "Checkout" to proceed to the next step of checkout
